About Eklavya Hospital

Dedicated to Healing, Maternal Wellbeing & Trauma Care in Vyara

Eklavya Multispeciality Hospital / Maternity Home (Trauma Center) is an established medical landmark in Vyara, Tapi District, Gujarat. Built on the principles of patient-first care, surgical excellence, and prompt emergency response, the hospital serves as a trusted healthcare sanctuary for families across South Gujarat.

Under the visionary leadership of Dr. Sonal Padvi Chaudhari (Dr. Sonal G. Padvi) and Dr. Kunal Gamit, both senior practitioners bringing over seven years of specialized clinical rigor, Eklavya offers high-precision orthopaedic interventions, advanced laparoscopic and maternity services, and robust emergency triage.

Department of Gynaecology & Obstetrics

Comprehensive women’s health services spanning prenatal care, maternity birthing suites, fertility guidance, and minimally invasive laparoscopic interventions.

Key Clinical Procedures & Care:

  • Normal & Cesarean (LSCS) Deliveries: Safe delivery suites and 24/7 obstetric emergency response.
  • Gynecological Laparoscopic Surgeries: Minimally invasive keyhole procedures with faster recovery.
  • Total Laparoscopic Hysterectomy (TLH): Advanced laparoscopic surgical removal for fibroid uterus.
  • Primary Infertility Care & Couple Counseling: Compassionate diagnostic workup and fertility management.
  • PCOS / PCOD Management: Tailored hormonal rebalancing, cycle regulation, and lifestyle protocols.
  • Pap Smear Screenings: Early cervical cancer detection, routine swabs, and preventive diagnostics.

Department of Orthopaedics & Spine

Expert surgical and non-surgical musculoskeletal care dedicated to bone trauma, joint preservation, spine evaluations, and rapid fracture restoration.

Key Clinical Procedures & Care:

  • Comprehensive Orthopaedic Surgical Procedures: Corrective surgeries for joint, ligament, and bone pathologies.
  • Specialized Spinal Consultations: Lumbar and cervical evaluation, disk disorders, and back rehabilitation.
  • Trauma Care for Bone Fractures & Injuries: Emergency plaster casting, internal fixation, and reduction.
  • Joint Mobility & Arthritis Therapy: Targeted medical therapy and joint pain management programs.
  • Post-Trauma Rehabilitation: Collaborative physiotherapy ensuring complete functional regain.
